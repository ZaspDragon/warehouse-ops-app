Average Productivity Per Person

Employee names are automatically added to the table from the uploaded workbook. Pulled per day = Pulled ÷ 5; Checked per day = Checked ÷ 5. For multiple uploaded weeks, average each employee’s totals across the weeks in which they appear. Average/day = total net lines ÷ (5 × number of weeks the employee appears). Every report represents one 5-day workweek. Net figures are taken directly from the Excel report, including any penalties or adjustments.
